Your own website

Enquiries off your site
land in here.

Point the contact form you already have at the address Attaché gives you. Nothing on your site gets rebuilt. An enquiry is saved the second it arrives, then goes to the right person by territory, or to whoever you named as the backstop.

Nothing gets invented

A form never makes up a company or a contact. It joins an enquiry to a company you already have when the email domain or the name matches exactly, and it becomes a contact when a person presses the button.

What they ticked has a date on it

Name a campaign on the form and everyone who fills it in joins that list, with what they agreed to, the day, and the form it came from. That is the bit you want on hand if anybody ever asks why you emailed them.

A QR code for the stand

Same form, printed. People scan it at the event, pick up the pen, and they are on the list before you have packed the car.
